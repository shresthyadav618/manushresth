I don't think I wanted a website.

At least, I don't think that's what I wanted.

I wanted somewhere to put things.

A thought that showed up at 2 AM and disappeared by breakfast.

A photograph I took because something about the light felt right.

A sentence I wrote and couldn't explain.

Something I learned.

Something I forgot.

Something I wish I hadn't.

Life moves ridiculously fast when you don't pay attention to it.

Days become weeks, weeks become months, and suddenly you're trying to remember what you actually did with the year.

Maybe this is my way of resisting that.

Not dramatically.

Just a little.

A small corner of the internet where things can stay.

I don't know what this place will become.

Maybe nothing.

Maybe a lot.

For now, I'm just leaving the door open.
