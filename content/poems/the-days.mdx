---
title: "The Days"
date: "2026-14-08"
description: "A reminder that ordinary days are still your life."
---

One day

you'll miss

the completely ordinary morning

you didn't think

was worth remembering.

The walk.

The bad coffee.

The song you played

without thinking.

The sunlight

on the floor.

Someone laughing

in another room.

We keep waiting

for life to become

something worth writing about.

And meanwhile

it is happening.

Quietly.

Everywhere.
