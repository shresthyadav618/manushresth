---
title: "2 AM"
date: "2026-09-02"
description: "For the nights when the world gets quieter and your head gets louder."
---

At 2 AM

the room becomes honest.

No notifications.

No conversations.

No one asking

how you're doing.

Just the ceiling,

the fan,

and every thought

you successfully avoided

during the day.

Some nights

I want an answer.

Some nights

I just want the night

to end.

But sometimes

I think

the quiet isn't asking me

to solve anything.

Maybe it's just asking me

to stay.
