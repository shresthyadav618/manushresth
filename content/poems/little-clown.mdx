---
title: "Oh Little Clown"
date: "2026-09-03"
description: "nothing profound, just a few things I felt"
---


If heaven ever gave me one power,
I'd spend it in someone's darkest hour
sit beside the lonely
when the whole world walks away,
and remind them softly,
*you don't have to be okay.*

Perhaps that's because I know
how loud the quiet can become,
how a person can disappear
without ever leaving the room.

I've always wondered
what it would feel like
to be seen without explanation—
no mask,
no *I'm fine,*
no carefully edited version
of a life.

Just the unfinished parts.
The cracks.
The things I haven't learned to name.

Maybe that's how a damaged soul
becomes something beautiful
not by hiding every scar,
but by letting the light
find its way through them.

I think solitude became my home
because it never asked me
to perform.

Take my strength if you need it.
Take every victory
and call it yours.
Just leave me the quiet
I've found too much of myself there
to give it away.

And if I cannot bend
the will of heaven above,
then I'll raise a little hell
for the things I still love.

But I'm learning
not every battle is mine,
not every door is meant to open,
and sometimes timing
is simply another name for life
telling you to move on.

Somewhere underneath it all
there is still a little clown
tired, ridiculous,
trying his hardest,
giving what he can.

So don't be so hard on yourself,
little clown.

Take off the painted smile.
Put the red nose down.
The world will survive
if you stop performing for a while.

Rest.

Let the night pass
without asking it for answers.

And when the dust eventually settles
over this chest of mine,
I hope it carries nothing heavier
than the lessons I've collected.

Because perhaps the point
was never to leave untouched.

Perhaps we're meant to become
a mosaic of everything
that tried to break us—

still imperfect,
still unfinished,
still here.

And maybe that's enough.
